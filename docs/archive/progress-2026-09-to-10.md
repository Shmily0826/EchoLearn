- **`ECHO-20260924-FOLLOW-ANCHOR-V1` (focus raised above centre, and the learner's scroll stops being fought).** Feedback after `ECHO-20260924-RESTORE-CLOCK-V1` landed: the highlight and the scrolling work, but "还是很容易被拉走", and "滚动聚焦位置能不能中间往上一点点". Both reports are properties of the same comfort band, and they cannot be satisfied together by moving its edges: with minimum-delta following the line **rests exactly on the band's lower edge**, so any drift downward crosses it and the next cue drags the learner back — and raising that edge to lift the resting place only shrinks the slack further. The band is therefore replaced by an **anchor**: the list moves by the least amount that keeps the line's bottom at 45% of the pane, which follows a line at a time and parks the focus just above the middle. The grab was then fixed where it actually lived. `FOLLOW_RESUME_GRACE_MS` stays 6s but stops being a timer: a timer cannot express "while they are still reading it", because a cue boundary — the only moment the hold is tested — can arrive long after any timer has run out, and a sentence longer than the window ended with the list moving under someone who had not touched anything. The manual hold is now a timestamp evaluated at that boundary: **kept for as long as the line is inside the pane, and otherwise honoured for 6s from the last input**, so scrolling far ahead still releases and the transcript cannot detach itself for the rest of the lesson. Applied identically to `TranscriptViewer` and `MobileTranscriptPanel`. Falsified per property, each revert grep-verified first: anchor back at 0.75 reports `the line rests at 75% down the pane`; dropping the in-view extension reports `the list moved 266px once the fixed window expired` — which is the reported symptom, measured. The E2E now pins the resting place by median over a steady run (a single sample can catch the line mid-drift) rather than by a per-cue bound.

**A second defect in the same report, found by measuring on Production rather than locally.** The first Production run of the new code showed the anchor working (the line's bottom resting at 45-46% of the pane across 14 samples) but the hold **never engaging at all** — a learner scroll was dragged back exactly the distance it had been moved. Cause: `TranscriptViewer` attached its `wheel` / `touchmove` listeners by resolving the scroll pane **through the active row** (`activeLineRef.current.closest('.overflow-y-auto')`) in an effect keyed on `[handleUserScroll, lines]`. The synthetic fixtures all started their first cue at `0`, which mounts an active row at t=0 and let the listeners be reached through it — the same "the fixture satisfies the property by accident" trap already recorded for the 40 s WAV. Fixed by resolving the pane from the component's own root element, which exists whether or not a line is active.
